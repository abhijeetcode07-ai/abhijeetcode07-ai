## About Me

I'm an 18-year-old self-taught AI engineer based in India, currently pursuing a **BCA at IITM Janakpuri (affiliated to GGSIPU)** while building real-world skills in AI/ML from the ground up alongside it.

I chose this path with full clarity — not because it was easy or conventional, but because I believe AI is the most impactful field to be building in right now. No top college, no shortcuts — just consistent daily learning, real projects, and a roadmap I rebuilt for 2026 to reflect where the field actually is: agentic systems, retrieval, and evaluation, not just model training.

My focus area is **NLP and Generative AI** — building retrieval-augmented and agentic systems, fine-tuning large language models, and creating AI-powered applications that are actually evaluated, not just demoed.
